\begin{abstract}
  The J.83B Annex~B standard defines the North American downstream
  physical layer for digital video transmission over hybrid
  fiber-coaxial cable networks, specifying a 256-QAM single-carrier
  modulation scheme at 5.360537\,Msym/s within a 6\,MHz channel
  allocation. This thesis presents the design, implementation, and
  verification of a J.83B Annex~B 256-QAM transmitter on the Xilinx
  Zynq~UltraScale+ RFSoC~4x2 FPGA, targeting delivery of a modulated
  RF signal at 177\,MHz from an MPEG-TS input stream.

  The system partitions across the three RFSoC~4x2 subsystems:
  the Processing System handles control and data movement; the
  Programmable Logic implements the full J.83B pipeline in custom
  SystemVerilog at 250\,MHz; and the RF Data Converter performs
  interpolation, digital upconversion, and DAC conversion to produce
  the RF output.

  Verification confirmed a continuous gap-free output stream under
  sustained operation, a correct RF signal at 177\,MHz with spectral
  shape consistent with the J.83B specification, and correct analog
  signal path behavior via an RF Data Converter isolation test.

  Consumer device lock was not achieved. Thirteen candidate hypotheses
  were systematically investigated and eliminated, localizing the
  failure to the protocol layer. Six hypotheses remain open as
  directions for future work.
\end{abstract}

\begin{biosketch}
  Sathwik Sudhindranath received the B.E.\ degree in Electronics
  and Communication Engineering from Visvesvaraya Technological
  University, India, in 2021. Following graduation, he worked at
  Tata Consultancy Services Ltd.\ as a Software Developer for
  three and a half years. In 2025, he entered the Electrical
  Engineering graduate program at The University of Texas at
  Dallas, Richardson, Texas, with a concentration in Computing
  Systems. During his graduate studies, he conducted research
  under Dr.\ Benjamin Carrion Schaefer on an industry-sponsored
  project with ANSpect, implementing a J.83B Annex~B 256-QAM
  transmitter on the Xilinx Zynq~UltraScale+ RFSoC~4x2 FPGA.
  He expects to receive the M.S.\ degree in December 2026.
